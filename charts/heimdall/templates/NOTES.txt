  ⚠ CHECK WHETHER THAT CLASS RECLAIMS OR DELETES — one command, now rather than later:

      kubectl get storageclass -o custom-columns=NAME:.metadata.name,RECLAIM:.reclaimPolicy

  On k3s the default is `local-path` with reclaimPolicy: Delete, so a
  `kubectl delete pvc` destroys this volume and everything on it.

  What that costs you as a JOINING node: `bifrost.skey` is the node identity, and
  losing it means RE-REGISTERING (an on-chain tx you can repeat) — not lost funds.
  The current cycle's DKG share goes with it; the trie files are recomputable.
  `federation-key.json`, whose loss WOULD be permanent, is produced by the initial
  federation ceremony and is not on a joining node's volume.

  To get `Retain` declaratively, commit a StorageClass with
  `reclaimPolicy: Retain` and set `persistence.storageClass` to it.
  To repair a volume that ALREADY exists on the wrong class (keeps the data):

      kubectl patch pv <name> -p '{"spec":{"persistentVolumeReclaimPolicy":"Retain"}}'
